About NGHI

Building a healthier future through knowledge, research, community engagement and practical action.

Who We Are

NextGen Health Initiative (NGHI) is a youth-led public health organization committed to making health information more understandable, accessible and useful to individuals and communities.

We work at the intersection of health education, research, community engagement and public health advocacy.

Our approach is practical and community-focused: we identify health challenges, communicate evidence clearly and support actions that can contribute to healthier communities.

Our Vision

A healthier society where individuals and communities have the knowledge, opportunities and support needed to make informed health decisions.

Our Mission

To promote better health outcomes through health education, evidence-informed research, community engagement and collaboration.

Meet the Founder

The person behind the vision and development of NextGen Health Initiative.

Olawuwo Idris Adeniyi

Founder & Team Lead, NextGen Health Initiative

Idris Adeniyi is a public health professional with a background in Anatomy and a growing focus on public health research, health education, epidemiology and community health.

He founded NextGen Health Initiative with the goal of creating a platform where health information, research and community action can come together to address practical health challenges.

His work spans public health research, health communication, evidence synthesis and community-focused initiatives.
