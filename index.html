OUR COMMITMENT

Making health knowledge useful, accessible and relevant to everyday life.

GET TO KNOW US

Health challenges require more than awareness. They require action.

NextGen Health Initiative (NGHI) is a youth-led public health organisation committed to advancing health education, research, advocacy and community health development.

We recognise that improving health outcomes requires informed individuals, stronger communities, accessible health information and the effective use of evidence.

Through our programmes, projects and collaborations, we work towards addressing public health challenges and encouraging meaningful participation in health development.

What Drives Our Work

Our commitment is guided by a shared vision of healthier people and stronger communities.

01

Our Mission

To contribute to improved health outcomes through public health research, accessible health education, advocacy, community engagement and strategic collaboration.

03

Our Approach

We bring together research, education, community participation and partnerships to support practical responses to public health challenges.
