WHO WE ARE

Working Towards Healthier Communities

NextGen Health Initiative (NGHI) is a youth-led public health organisation committed to improving health and wellbeing through evidence-based interventions, research, education and community engagement.

We recognise that improving public health requires more than awareness. It requires reliable evidence, meaningful community participation, effective communication and collaboration between individuals and institutions.

Through our programmes and initiatives, we aim to contribute to practical solutions to public health challenges while creating opportunities for young people to participate in meaningful health development activities.
